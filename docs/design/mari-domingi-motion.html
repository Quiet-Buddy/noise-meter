What changes
  • Tontorra: the hook does the acting. It bounces and curls tight when happy, straightens and twitches when worried, flops over when sad, stands bolt upright and trembles when too loud, and nods forward when asleep. A pat makes it boing.
  • Body: she bobs and sways when happy, and her skirt swings against the sway. Sad sinks her down; too loud makes her shake.
  • Hands and basket: happy holds an apple up and waves it, worried puts a hand to her cheek, sad lets the basket hang low, too loud claps both hands over her ears and drops the basket so the apples roll away.
  • Face: blinks and small glances; a sweat drop when worried, a tear that keeps running when sad, gritted teeth when too loud.
  • Where you tap: her tontorra unrolls its hook and springs it back; her basket pops an apple up and catches it; anywhere else she leans her head into the pat, blushes and hearts float up from your finger.
  • Combos: hearts grow with each quick pat. 3 pats: she tosses an apple from the basket and catches it, watching it all the way. 6: a crouch, a big jump with arms up and a burst of petals on landing. 9: an aurresku-style dance, with hop-kicks on alternate legs and arms held high.
  • Not calm: tapping her when it's loud doesn't reward the noise. She puts a finger to her lips and soft "shh" puffs drift away. Asleep, one eye peeks open and she dozes off again.
  • Asleep: a crescent moon and twinkling stars. No Z's, so nothing needs translating.
