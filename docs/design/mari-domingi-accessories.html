The proposals

Robin on the hook txantxangorria

Head · 3,300 · top reward

Chosen

A little robin perches on the curl of her tontorra and lives through the room with her.

  • Hops on the curl when she's happy
  • Flutters up off the hook when it gets loud
  • Flies away when it's too loud, and comes back when the room calms
  • Tucks its head in to sleep
  • A tap on the tontorra startles it into a flutter

Why: Turns quiet into something children want to protect: keep it calm or the bird flies off.

Watch out: Small at icon size; it rides on the hook, so it moves with every tontorra reaction.

Ankle bells dancers' bells

Feet · not chosen

A leather strap of brass bells over each abarka lace, like the bells Basque dancers wear.

  • Jingle marks when she dances, jumps or trembles at too loud
  • Still and quiet when she's calm or asleep

Why: Uses the empty feet slot and shows off the 9-pat dance. Purely wordless: the jingle is drawn, not written.

Watch out: Hardly noticeable while she stands still; it shines in motion.

Basket of presents opariak

Body · not chosen

Small wrapped presents instead of apples, because she brings gifts on Christmas Eve.

  • Held up instead of the apple when she's happy
  • Tossed and caught in the 3-pat trick
  • Rolls out of the dropped basket at too loud

Why: Tells her story as a gift-bringer at a glance.

Watch out: Swaps out the apples everywhere, and the apples are part of who she is. Small boxes read less clearly than apples.

Lamb arkumea

Body · not chosen

A lamb from her flock sits at her feet, facing her, and feels everything she feels.

  • Bounces when she's happy
  • Ears droop when she's sad
  • Crouches and trembles, eyes squeezed shut, when it's too loud
  • Curls up and sleeps when she does

Why: The richest reactions of any accessory, and it fits her as a shepherdess.

Watch out: Widens the picture and is nearly a second character to build and maintain in the app.

Lauburu brooch lauburua

Neck · not chosen

The Basque four-headed whirl in gold, pinned at her collar. It sits above the zapia when both are worn.

  • Static: it doesn't react to moods

Why: The most recognisable Basque symbol, and tiny, so it never gets in the way.

Watch out: Too small to notice on a phone, and it has no animation.
