Mari Domingi

Directions for the fifth character, drawn in the same chunky-outline style as Buddy, Mochi, Pip and Astro. Each shows how she reacts to the noise level, without any words. E, built from your reference images, is the newest.

  • Wrapped tontorra with a curled hook
  • Laced bodice, white apron, red skirt
  • Apples, ideally roasted
  • Shepherdess who reads the moon
  • Olentzero's equal, not his wife
E

From the references

Built on nine reference images: parade costumes, posters, storybook and cartoon versions, and a sewn toca on a mannequin. This is what they have in common.

  • Wrapped tontorra: white cloth wound round her head in a few chunky bands that spiral upwards. It sits low and wide over her forehead and rounds off gently instead of narrowing to a point.
  • Curled hook: the last of the cloth rises out of the top and curls forward into a spiral. That hook is the single most recognisable part.
  • Hair: most versions show her as a warm, grandmotherly figure, with grey hair peeking out under the toca. Some wrap her face completely; that's kept as a variant.
  • Outfit: the most common one is a laced burgundy bodice over a white blouse, a long white apron and a red skirt. Abarkak laced up the leg in every version.
  • Basket: on her arm, full of apples.

Moods

  • Happy: holds up an apple, eyes squeezed in a big smile, hook curled tight, apple-blossom petals.
  • Fine: basket on her arm, gentle smile.
  • Worried: hand to her cheek, raised brows, a bead of sweat; the hook starts to straighten.
  • Sad: a tear, head and basket sink, and the hook flops over limp.
  • Too loud: hands over her ears, the hook shoots straight up, and the basket drops with apples rolling out.
  • Asleep: eyes closed, the tontorra nods forward, moon and stars.
  • Pat: eyes shut with delight, deep blush, floating hearts.

Silhouette test

Filled in solid at app-icon sizes. Nothing else in the cast has this outline.

E variants

The references differ on her age, how much hair shows and her colours. The toca and the apple basket stay the same in all of them.

Earlier directions

Style ideas (on direction A)

Unlockable outfits, like the other characters have. The headdress stays white in all of them so she's always recognisable.

The headdress does the acting

  • The folded tip works like the kitten's ears: it perks up when it's getting loud, shoots straight up at too loud, and flops over when she sleeps.
  • It's also her silhouette. Even as a tiny icon, nobody else in the app has that shape.

Wordless cues

  • Too loud: hands over ears, red sound waves.
  • Asleep: crescent moon and stars, since she reads the moon.
  • Pat or reward: a roasted apple with a curl of steam, or apple-blossom petals.
