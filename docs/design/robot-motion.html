Astro mood motion

Meet Astro the robot, hovering over the floor on a little thruster. Tap a mood and watch Astro move into it, in any order. When the room is calm, tap Astro (or press P) for a pat. Number keys 1–6 switch moods.

What changes
  • Hover: Astro bobs over the floor on a thruster flame. Happy floats high and sways, sad sinks low, and asleep lands with the flame off.
  • Screen face: glowing eyes and mouth. Happy shows ^ ^ eyes and cheek lights, worried shows slanted brows and glances, too loud squeezes the eyes shut and the mouth turns to static.
  • Antenna light goes yellow when calm, flickers amber when worried, dims blue when sad and turns red when too loud. It droops when sad and rattles when it's too loud.
  • Hands float beside the body: they wave when happy, wring close when worried, hang low when sad and clamp over the ear discs when too loud.
  • Pats: one pat gives heart eyes and an antenna boing. Three quick pats make Astro spin once on the spot, so you see the back hatch go by. Six make a jump with a double spin that ends in a burst of sparkles. Nine send Astro flying one lap around the room, shrinking into the distance and coming back to the front.
  • Asleep: the screen dims and a battery icon charges. No Z's, so nothing needs translating.
