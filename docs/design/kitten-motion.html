What changes
  • Fur grows from smooth to bristling for too loud.
  • Ears tilt back when worried, droop when sad, fold flat when too loud.
  • Tail curls up, droops, bottle-brushes, or wraps the paws asleep.
  • Eyes and mouth morph; tears, sweat, sparkles and Z’s fade in.
