Duck mood motion

Meet Pip the duck (Pío in Spanish, Txito in Basque), floating on his pond. Tap a mood and watch him move into it, in any order. When the room is calm, tap Pip (or press P) to pat him. Number keys 1–6 switch moods.

What changes
  • Head tilts up and bobs when happy, stretches up when worried, droops when sad and tucks in to sleep.
  • Wings flap when happy, hang when sad, and clamp over both sides of his head when too loud.
  • Feathers ruffle and shiver when it gets too loud.
  • Tail perks up and wags when happy or patted, droops when sad.
  • Bill opens in a smile or a quack and droops when he’s unhappy; tears, sweat, sparkles and Z’s fade in.
  • Water: he bobs on the pond, ripples spread out and his feet paddle underneath, faster when he’s upset.
