**Full description**

Quiet Buddy turns your phone into a gentle noise monitor. Instead of a confusing number, you get Buddy, a small character whose mood follows the noise around it.

When the room is calm, Buddy is happy. When it gets louder, Buddy gets worried, then sad, and eventually covers its ears. Buddy waits a moment before changing mood, so it doesn't flicker at every small sound.

Useful anywhere silence matters: classrooms, libraries, study rooms, offices, waiting rooms, shared homes or a quiet corner for reading.

What you can do:
- Choose a noise limit that suits the place: Quiet time, Everyday or Playtime.
- Set quiet hours. During those hours Buddy becomes stricter, handy for evenings or nap time.
- Look back at History to see how noisy the day was and when the loudest moments happened.
- Take on a 30-second Quiet Challenge and see whether the room can stay calm.
- Hear sound alerts when it gets loud: a soft tone when Buddy is worried, stronger when Buddy is sad. You can turn them off in Settings.
- Keep listening with the screen off. A small notification shows Buddy's mood, and you can pause listening from it at any time.
- Calibrate the microphone against a sound meter you own, to improve accuracy on your phone.
- Use it in English, Spanish or Basque. It follows your phone's language.

Your privacy:
- Nothing is recorded. Buddy measures how loud things are. It does not save audio or store what anyone says.
- Nothing leaves your phone. The app has no internet access, so your history stays on your device.
- No account, no sign-up, no ads.

Permissions:
- Microphone: needed to measure the noise level.
- Notifications: used to show Buddy's mood while it runs in the background.

A note on accuracy:
Quiet Buddy gives a good general idea of how loud a space is. It is not a certified sound level meter, and phone microphones vary from model to model. Do not use it for legal, medical or workplace safety measurements.
