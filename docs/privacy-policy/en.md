# Quiet Buddy Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 2026-11-11

## Who we are

Quiet Buddy is a small open-source project that makes an Android app. You can learn more at [quiet-buddy.github.io](https://quiet-buddy.github.io).

Questions about this policy? Contact us at: CONTACT_EMAIL_PLACEHOLDER

## What the app accesses and why

- **Microphone (RECORD_AUDIO).** Quiet Buddy uses the microphone only to measure how loud the room is. It does not record, save, or play back audio, and it does not try to understand speech.
- **Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS).** Used to show Buddy's current mood while the app runs in the background, and to alert you when it gets loud.
- **Foreground service (FOREGROUND_SERVICE, FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MICROPHONE).** Lets listening keep running while the app is in the background. You can pause listening at any time from the notification.

Quiet Buddy does not request access to your contacts, location, photos, files, or camera.

## What is stored, and where

Quiet Buddy stores only the following, and only on your device:

- **Noise level history:** the loudness readings used for your history screens, kept in a local database (Room).
- **Settings:** your noise limits, quiet hours, calibration, and other preferences, kept locally (DataStore).

The app has no internet access. Your data is never sent anywhere.

## What is shared

Nothing. Quiet Buddy does not share, sell, or transfer any data to anyone. It has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics, and no third-party software development kits (SDKs).

## Data retention and deletion

Your history and settings stay on your device until you remove them. Uninstalling Quiet Buddy deletes all of its local data. The app is set not to be included in device backups, so no copy of your data is kept elsewhere.

## Children

Quiet Buddy does not collect personal data from anyone, including children. It is designed for use in shared spaces such as classrooms and family homes, and it does not ask for names, ages, or any other personal information.

## Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date at the top of this page and publish the new version here. Please check back from time to time.
